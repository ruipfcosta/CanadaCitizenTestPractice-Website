Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

I take your privacy very seriously. Canada Citizen Test Practice is built so you can study without giving up any personal information. This policy explains what the app and this website do, and don't do, with your data.

The short version

  • No account, sign-up, name or email address is needed to use the app.
  • Your progress, test results and settings are stored only on your device.
  • The app shows no ads and does not track you across other apps or websites.
  • Anonymous usage events are collected through a privacy-friendly analytics service, only to understand how the app is used and to improve it.
  • Your data is never sold.

Data stored on your device

Your answers, mock test results, mistakes, progress and app settings are saved locally on your iPhone, iPad or Mac. This data is not sent to me or to any server, and it is deleted when you delete the app.

Usage analytics

The app uses a privacy-friendly analytics service to log anonymous events, for example that a mock test was started or a screen was opened. These events help me understand which features are useful and where the app can be improved.

The events do not include your name, email address, contacts, location or any other personal details, and they are not used for advertising or to track you across other companies' apps and websites.

In-app purchase

Premium features are unlocked with a one-off in-app purchase, which is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. I never see or receive your payment details. Apple's handling of your purchase is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

This website

This website does not use cookies or analytics. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log your IP address for security purposes, as described in GitHub's Privacy Statement. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google's servers when you visit.

Children

The app is a study aid for adults preparing for the Canadian citizenship test. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because the app does not collect personal information that identifies you, there is normally no personal data held about you to access, correct or delete. If you have any questions or concerns, please get in touch. If you are in Canada, you also have the right to complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

Contact
